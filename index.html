Vantage Clarity™ vs. Traditional Retrofit And MEES Reports

Traditional Retrofit and MEES Compliance
Traditional retrofit strategies are commonly underwritten before installer validation and buildability are confirmed, with delivery certainty emerging late in the process.
Conceptual. Strategies are typically high level and indicative. Asset-specific scope, buildability and true cost only emerge after further design and professional team input.
Advisory. Early retrofit routes are advisory only, with accountability for cost accuracy and outcome excluded. Caveated estimates leave material risk with you at decision stage.
Deferred. Installer input and pricing is obtained late in the process. You wait months before contractors are engaged to provide pricing and risk insight.
Vantage Clarity™
Vantage Clarity™ lets you test, price and underwrite retrofit strategies early, using real delivery insight rather than high-level advice.
Exhaustive. All viable retrofit routes are exhaustively assessed, with asset-specific equipment selected, and scope priced by the installers who will actually deliver the work.
Specialist. We price and deliver retrofit strategies as Principal Contractor, retaining delivery and EPC risk, rather than operating in an advisory role.
Pre-capital. Decision-grade cost detail and EPC modelling are reached before capital deployment, so you can underwrite with confidence. Clarity™ before capital.
Enhanced feasibility. A significant reduction in investment risk. Clarity™ before capital, always.
